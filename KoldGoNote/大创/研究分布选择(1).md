## 多元 T 分布

The multivariate (MV) Student's t distribution is a multivariate continuous distribution that generalizes the one-dimensional Student's t distribution.
（可以生成一维的学生 t 分布）
## T 分布
**更加厚尾**

一个服从 **标准 t 分布**（n 个自由度）的随机变量 $X$ 可以写成一个比的形式 (ratio)
$$
X=\frac{Z}{{\sqrt{ \mathcal{X}_{n}^{2} /n}}}
$$
 - What does it means>
 - $Z$ has a  standard normal distribution
 - $\mathcal{X}^{2}_{n}$ has a Chi-Square distribution
 - $Z$ and $\mathcal{X}_{n}^{2}$ are independent of each other
	- 自由度为 $n$ 的卡方分布，除以 $n$，则其有一个伽马分布
即
$$
X=\frac{Z}{\sqrt{ G }}
$$

#### Non-standard 
线性变换。

$$
X=\mu+\sigma {\frac{Z}{\sqrt{ \mathcal{X}^{2}_{n}/n }}}
$$

#### Distribution 详细解读

$X$ 有一个 standard Student's t distribution with $n$ degrees of freedom **iff** its probability density function is 
$$
f_{X}(x)=c\left( 1+\frac{x^{2}}{n} \right)^{{-(n+1)/2}}
$$
$c$ is a constant
$$
c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ n }}{\frac{1}{B\left( \frac{n}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right)}}
$$
$B$ is the beta function

$$
B(x,y)=\frac{{\Gamma(x)\Gamma(y)}}{\Gamma(x+y)}
$$


记号:
$$
X\sim T(\mu,\sigma^{2},n)
$$


#### 数值特征

对于服从 Student's t random variable $X$，且 $n>1$，有
$$
E[X]=\mu
$$

方差: $X$ is well-defined only for $n>2$ and it is equal to
$$
Var[X]=\frac{n}{n-2}\sigma^{2}
$$
- **这里值得注意的是**，$n$ 越小，我们说越厚尾。


### Chi-square distribution

t 分布的定义用到了卡方分布，所以也介绍一下。
#### Definition
**Chi-square random variables** are characterized as follows.

Definition Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a **continuous random variable**. Let its support be the set of positive real numbers:

Let $n\in \mathbb{N}$. We say that X has a Chi-square distribution with **n degrees of freedom** if and only if its probability density function is
$$
\left.f_X(x)=\left\{
\begin{array}
{ll}cx^{n/2-1}\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}x\right) & ifx\in R_X \\
0 & ifx\not\in R_X
\end{array}\right.\right.
$$
where $c$ is a constant:
$$
c=\frac{1}{2^{n/2}\Gamma(n/2)}
$$
- consider $\Gamma(t)=\int_{0}^{\infty} x^{t-1}e^{ -x } \, dx$
- 所以该密度函数符合概率分布的归一性


- 记号
$$
X\sim\mathcal{X}^{2}(n)
$$


## RN 导数

$t$ 分布做建议分布，正态分布做原分布。
- $f(x)$ 标准正态分布
- $f(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\pi }}e^{-x^{2}/2}$

- $g(x)$ 自由度为 $\nu$. 标准 t 分布
- $g(x)={\frac{\Gamma((v+1)/2)}{\sqrt{ v\pi\Gamma(\nu/2) }}\left( 1+\frac{x^{2}}{\nu} \right)^{-(v+1)/2}}$

**似然比**

$$
L(x)=\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}= \frac{\Gamma(\nu/2)}{\Gamma((\nu+1)/2)}\sqrt{\frac{\nu}{2}}\cdot e^{-x^{2}/2}\cdot(1+\frac{x^{2}}{\nu})^{(\nu+1)/2}
$$


### 实际尝试：使用多元 t 分布

#### 1. 原始分布 \(p (x)\)（多元正态）

$$
p(x) = \frac{1}{ (2\pi)^{d/2} |\Sigma|^{1/2} } 
       \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}(x-\mu)^\top \Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)\right)
$$

---

#### 2. 提议分布 \(q (x)\)（多元 t 分布）

$$
q(x) = \frac{ \Gamma\!\left(\frac{\nu+d}{2}\right) }{ \Gamma\!\left(\frac{\nu}{2}\right) (\nu\pi)^{d/2} |\Sigma_q|^{1/2} } 
       \left( 1 + \frac{1}{\nu} (x-\mu_q)^\top \Sigma_q^{-1}(x-\mu_q) \right)^{-\frac{\nu+d}{2}}
$$

---

#### 3 . RN 导数（似然比权重）

$$
w(x) = \frac{p(x)}{q(x)}
$$

取对数形式：

$$
\log w(x) = \log p(x) - \log q(x)
$$

---

####  4. 二维示例（资产收益 $r_i$ 和市场收益 $r_m$）

$$
w(r_i, r_m) = 
\frac{
    \frac{1}{2\pi |\Sigma|^{1/2}} 
    \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}(x-\mu)^\top \Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)\right)
}{
    \frac{ \Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{\nu+2}{2}\right) }{ \Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{\nu}{2}\right) (\nu\pi)^{1} |\Sigma_q|^{1/2} } 
    \left(1 + \frac{1}{\nu}(x-\mu_q)^\top \Sigma_q^{-1}(x-\mu_q)\right)^{-(\nu+2)/2}
}
$$
### 代码实验
```python
def mes_convergence_compare(asset_returns, market_returns, alpha=0.05,
                            n_samples_list=None, kappa_list=None, K=50, df_list=None):
    """
    比较: 正态偏移提议分布 vs. t 提议分布的收敛性能
    """
    if n_samples_list is None:
        n_samples_list = np.logspace(3, 5, 6, dtype=int)
    if kappa_list is None:
        kappa_list = [-3, -2, -1, 0]
    if df_list is None:
        df_list = [3, 5, 10]  # 不同厚尾程度的 t 分布
	# 前导知识：自由度越低(degree of freedom)，越厚尾

    results = {}
    data, mu, Sigma, VaR, _ = prepare.prepare_data_for_mes(asset_returns, market_returns, alpha=alpha)
    sigma_m = np.sqrt(Sigma[1, 1])

    # Step 1: 大样本参考真值
    true_mes = importance_sampling_mes_stable(
        asset_returns, market_returns, alpha=alpha,
        n_samples=10 ** 6,
        proposal_mu=[mu[0], mu[1] + (-VaR)], k_soft=80
    )
    print(f"参考真值 MES ≈ {true_mes:.6f}")

    # Step 2: 正态提议分布实验
    for kappa in kappa_list:
        MES_estimates = []
        RMSE_list = []
        proposal_mu = np.array([mu[0], mu[1] + kappa * sigma_m])
        proposal_mu[0] = -VaR
        proposal_Sigma = Sigma.copy() + np.eye(2) * 1e-8

        for N in n_samples_list:
            errors = []
            for _ in range(K):
                est = importance_sampling_mes_stable(
                    asset_returns, market_returns,
                    alpha=alpha, n_samples=N,
                    proposal_mu=proposal_mu,
                    proposal_Sigma=proposal_Sigma,
                    k_soft=80
                )
                errors.append(est - true_mes)
            rmse = np.sqrt(np.mean(np.array(errors) ** 2))
            RMSE_list.append(rmse)
            MES_estimates.append(np.mean([true_mes + e for e in errors]))

        slope, _, _, _, _ = linregress(np.log(n_samples_list), np.log(RMSE_list))
        results[f"Normal_kappa={kappa}"] = {
            "RMSE": RMSE_list,
            "slope": slope
        }
        print(f"[Normal] kappa={kappa}, log-log 拟合斜率={slope:.4f}")

    # Step 3: t 分布提议分布实验
    for df in df_list:
        MES_estimates = []
        RMSE_list = []
        proposal_mu = np.array([mu[0], mu[1]])  # 不偏移，靠厚尾
        proposal_Sigma = Sigma.copy() + np.eye(2) * 1e-8

        for N in n_samples_list:
            errors = []
            for _ in range(K):
                est = importance_sampling_mes_tdist(
                    asset_returns, market_returns,
                    alpha=alpha, n_samples=N,
                    proposal_mu=proposal_mu,
                    proposal_Sigma=proposal_Sigma,
                    df=df, k_soft=80
                )
                errors.append(est - true_mes)
            rmse = np.sqrt(np.mean(np.array(errors) ** 2))
            RMSE_list.append(rmse)
            MES_estimates.append(np.mean([true_mes + e for e in errors]))

        slope, _, _, _, _ = linregress(np.log(n_samples_list), np.log(RMSE_list))
        results[f"t_df={df}"] = {
            "RMSE": RMSE_list,
            "slope": slope
        }
        print(f"[t-dist] df={df}, log-log 拟合斜率={slope:.4f}")

    # Step 4: 绘图对比
    plt.figure(figsize=(12, 6))
    markers = ["o", "s", "d", "^", "x", "*"]

    for i, (label, res) in enumerate(results.items()):
        plt.loglog(n_samples_list, res["RMSE"], marker=markers[i % len(markers)],
                   label=f"{label}, slope={res['slope']:.2f}")

    plt.xlabel("Sample size N")
    plt.ylabel("RMSE")
    plt.title("MES Importance Sampling: Normal Shift vs. t Proposal")
    plt.grid(True, which="both", ls="--")
    plt.legend()
    plt.show()

    return results

```
