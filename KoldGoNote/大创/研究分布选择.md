## T 分布
**更加厚尾**

一个服从 **标准 t 分布**（n 个自由度）的随机变量 $X$ 可以写成一个比的形式 (ratio)
$$
X=\frac{Z}{{\sqrt{ \mathcal{X}_{n}^{2} /n}}}
$$
 - What does it means>
 - $Z$ has a  standard normal distribution
 - $\mathcal{X}^{2}_{n}$ has a Chi-Square distribution
 - $Z$ and $\mathcal{X}_{n}^{2}$ are independent of each other
	- 自由度为 $n$ 的卡方分布，除以 $n$，则其有一个伽马分布
即
$$
X=\frac{Z}{\sqrt{ G }}
$$

#### Non-standard 
线性变换。

$$
X=\mu+\sigma {\frac{Z}{\sqrt{ \mathcal{X}^{2}_{n}/n }}}
$$

#### Distribution 详细解读

$X$ 有一个 standard Student's t distribution with $n$ degrees of freedom **iff** its probability density function is 
$$
f_{X}(x)=c\left( 1+\frac{x^{2}}{n} \right)^{{-(n+1)/2}}
$$
$c$ is a constant
$$
c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ n }}{\frac{1}{B\left( \frac{n}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right)}}
$$
$B$ is the beta function

$$
B(x,y)=\frac{{\Gamma(x)\Gamma(y)}}{\Gamma(x+y)}
$$


记号:
$$
X\sim T(\mu,\sigma^{2},n)
$$


#### 数值特征

对于服从 Student's t random variable $X$，且 $n>1$，有
$$
E[X]=\mu
$$

方差: $X$ is well-defined only for $n>2$ and it is equal to
$$
Var[X]=\frac{n}{n-2}\sigma^{2}
$$
- **这里值得注意的是**，$n$ 越小，我们说越厚尾。


### Chi-square distribution

t 分布的定义用到了卡方分布，所以也介绍一下。
#### Definition
**Chi-square random variables** are characterized as follows.

Definition Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a **continuous random variable**. Let its support be the set of positive real numbers:

Let $n\in \mathbb{N}$. We say that X has a Chi-square distribution with **n degrees of freedom** if and only if its probability density function is
$$
\left.f_X(x)=\left\{
\begin{array}
{ll}cx^{n/2-1}\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}x\right) & ifx\in R_X \\
0 & ifx\not\in R_X
\end{array}\right.\right.
$$
where $c$ is a constant:
$$
c=\frac{1}{2^{n/2}\Gamma(n/2)}
$$
- consider $\Gamma(t)=\int_{0}^{\infty} x^{t-1}e^{ -x } \, dx$
- 所以该密度函数符合概率分布的归一性


- 记号
$$
X\sim\mathcal{X}^{2}(n)
$$
